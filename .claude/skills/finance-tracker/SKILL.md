# Skill: Finance Tracker

Track income, expenses, and debt payoff progress. Goal: debt-free by December 31, 2026.

## When to use
- Monthly financial review
- Logging a payment, expense, or income
- Checking debt payoff progress
- Planning how to allocate a surplus

## Modes

### Monthly Review
1. Ask for this month's income (both jobs, any other sources)
2. Ask for fixed expenses (rent, phone, subscriptions, bills)
3. Ask for variable expenses (food, gas, entertainment, misc)
4. Calculate: income - expenses = surplus
5. Ask: how much of the surplus went toward debt?
6. Show: progress snapshot toward debt-free goal
7. Save summary to `references/finance/YYYY-MM-review.md`

### Debt Payoff Check-In
1. Ask for current balance on each debt
2. Calculate months remaining until Dec 31, 2026
3. Show required monthly payment per debt to hit the goal
4. Flag clearly: on track, behind, or ahead
5. Update `references/finance/debt-tracker.md`

### Quick Log
1. Ask what happened (income received, bill paid, random expense, etc.)
2. Append one line to `references/finance/log.md` with date and details

## Output Files
- Monthly reviews: `references/finance/YYYY-MM-review.md`
- Running log: `references/finance/log.md`
- Debt tracker: `references/finance/debt-tracker.md`
