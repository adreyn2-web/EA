# Skill: Research Routine

Run a focused research session and save the output where it belongs.

## When to use
- Learning a trading concept, strategy, or market mechanic
- Researching a business idea or entrepreneurship topic
- Any subject that feeds financial literacy, trading, or long-term growth

## Workflow

**Step 1 — Define the session**
Ask:
- What's the topic?
- What do you already know about it?
- What specific questions do you need answered?
- How deep? (quick 15-min hit vs. full deep dive)

**Step 2 — Run the research**

Quick-hit: Answer the 3 most important questions, bullet format, done.

Deep dive: Structured breakdown —
1. Overview (what it is, why it matters)
2. Key concepts (the things you need to actually understand it)
3. How it applies to Adreyn's situation specifically
4. What to read, watch, or do next

**Step 3 — Save to the right place**
- Trading topic → `projects/trading-bot/research/TOPIC.md`
- Business or entrepreneurship → `references/research/TOPIC.md`
- Health or fitness → `references/research/TOPIC.md`

**Step 4 — End with one action**
Always close with one concrete thing Adreyn can do with what he just learned. No research session ends without a next step.

## Output Format
Lead with the answer. Details follow. One next action at the end. No fluff.
