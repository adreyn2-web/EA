# Trading Bot / Day Trading Sidekick

The anchor project. An AI-powered co-pilot that sits alongside Adreyn while day trading.

**Status:** Building
**Goal:** Replace intuition gaps with data, build discipline through journaling, and compound knowledge over time.

---

## Core Modules

| Module | Folder | Purpose | Status |
|--------|--------|---------|--------|
| Trade Journal | `journal/` | Log every trade, track results, surface patterns | Backlog |
| Research | `research/` | Market notes, asset analysis, learning resources | Backlog |
| Strategies | `strategies/` | Documented setups and playbooks | Backlog |
| Performance | `performance/` | Stats, win rate, P&L tracking | Backlog |

## Build Order (recommended)
1. Trade journal — foundation for everything else
2. Strategies — document what you're trading and why
3. Performance tracking — measure what's working
4. Research — feed the machine with knowledge

## Key Dates
No hard deadlines. Building the right foundation first.
