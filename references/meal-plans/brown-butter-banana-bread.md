# Brown Butter Banana Bread (with Cream Cheese)

Elevated version — roasted bananas + browned butter for depth, cream cheese swirl or
topping for finish. All measurements by weight (scale) with volume as reference.

## Banana Bread

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Ripe bananas, roasted (mashed, peeled) | ~340g mashed | 3 bananas |
| Unsalted butter, browned | 140g | 10 tbsp |
| Eggs, room temp | ~100g (50g each) | 2 large |
| Dark brown sugar | 150g | 3/4 cup |
| Granulated sugar | 50g | 1/4 cup |
| Vanilla extract | 4g | 1 tsp |
| Buttermilk | 80g | 1/3 cup |
| Sour cream or Greek yogurt | 60g | 1/4 cup |
| All-purpose flour | 220g | 1 3/4 cups |
| Baking soda | 5g | 1 tsp |
| Salt | 3g | 1/2 tsp |
| Cinnamon | 1.5g | 1/2 tsp |
| Espresso powder (optional) | 0.5g | 1/4 tsp |
| Turbinado sugar, for topping | ~15g | — |
| Walnuts or dark chocolate (optional) | 60g (nuts) / 85g (choc) | 1/2 cup |

### Method
1. **Roast bananas**: unpeeled, 300°F for 15–20 min until skins are black and fully soft.
   Cool, peel, mash. Concentrates sugar beyond just "ripe."
2. **Brown the butter**: medium heat, swirl constantly until golden-brown and nutty
   (5–7 min). Pour into a bowl immediately to stop cooking. Cool 10 min.
3. Preheat oven to 350°F. Butter a 9x5 loaf pan, parchment overhang.
4. Whisk browned butter + both sugars until smooth. Whisk in eggs one at a time, then
   vanilla, buttermilk, sour cream. Fold in mashed roasted banana.
5. Whisk dry ingredients separately (flour, baking soda, salt, cinnamon, espresso powder).
6. Fold dry into wet just until no streaks remain — don't overmix. Fold in nuts/chocolate.
7. Pour into pan, sprinkle turbinado sugar on top. Bake 55–65 min, tent with foil at
   35 min if browning too fast. Done at ~205°F internal / tester with moist crumbs.
8. Cool in pan 15 min, then rack. Wait at least 1 hour before slicing — better next day.

## Cream Cheese Swirl (Option A — baked in)

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Cream cheese, room temp | 226g | 8 oz |
| Granulated sugar | 50g | 1/4 cup |
| Egg yolk | ~18g | 1 yolk |
| Vanilla extract | 2g | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | ~0.5g | pinch |

Beat until smooth. Layer: 2/3 of batter → cream cheese dollops → rest of batter →
remaining dollops. Swirl with a knife in loose figure-8s. Bake as above; may need an
extra 5–10 min, tent if top browns before center sets.

## Cream Cheese Topping (Option B — after baking)

| Ingredient | Weight | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Cream cheese, room temp | 113g | 4 oz |
| Unsalted butter, room temp | 42g | 3 tbsp |
| Powdered sugar | 90g | 3/4 cup |
| Vanilla extract | 2g | 1/2 tsp |
| Salt | ~0.5g | pinch |
| Milk or cream (to loosen) | 5–10g | 1–2 tsp |

Beat cream cheese + butter until smooth, add powdered sugar gradually, then vanilla
and salt. Spread on the *fully cooled* loaf only — warm bread melts it.

For the full elevated look: swirl (Option A) inside, thin drizzle of Option B on top
once cooled.
